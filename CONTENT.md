## Voice & tone

- **Warm but precise** — like a stylist who knows what to do the moment you sit down
- **No fluff superlatives** ("amazing," "best ever") — describe the experience
- **Second-person** ("you," "your hair") — never "our clients"
- **Active verbs** — "Book," "Get," "See"
- **Length:** Hero headline ≤ 8 words. Subhead ≤ 18 words. Service descriptions ≤ 14 words. Mobile-tight.
